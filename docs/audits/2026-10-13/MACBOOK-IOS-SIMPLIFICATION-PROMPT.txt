Apply the Limen Android 1.2.1 and 1.2.2 simplification to the iPhone app on this Mac

This is an implementation request. On 15 October 2026 the owner reviewed Android 1.2.0 on the phone and rejected the forms the audit remediation had produced. Two Android releases followed the same day, 1.2.1 (versionCode 6) and 1.2.2 (versionCode 7), and both are on the Play closed-testing track. Make the Swift app behave the same way, then verify it in the simulator and on the connected iPhone. Continue through implementation and verification rather than stopping at a plan.

This prompt comes after MACBOOK-IOS-PARITY-PROMPT.txt in the same folder. If that parity work has already been done on the Swift side, this is a removal pass over what it added. If it has not, fold these rules into it so the rejected controls are never built on iOS at all. Either way the Android reference is the head of main after the commit titled "Strip the sentence-long buttons, instruction text, lookup switch and controller block from the forms, put one-word keys at the bottom of each, and open the record from a hub door", not eb5c038. Check the Android head before reading anything.

1. The rules, which apply to every screen of the app

- A button is an icon and one or two words: Brouillon, Valider, Sauvegarder, Suivant. The screen's title says what is being validated; the button never repeats it, and it never names the session kind.
- The primary and the secondary action sit together at the bottom of their form, never in the middle of the page.
- No instructional copy under or above a field, no explanatory sentence under a screen title, and no text behind an info button that says what the screen is for. A label or a placeholder names the field.
- No switch for a behaviour that can simply happen. The swisstopo lookup runs whenever the phone is online and the field stays free to type when it is offline or nothing matches. The iPhone app never had the switch; make sure the parity work did not add one, and remove any disclosure sheet or consent step around the lookup.
- A disabled control that explains why it is disabled is not a control. Replace it with the action the person can take, or remove it.

At the bottom, one row with two keys: Brouillon with a save icon, outlined, which creates the record, saves it and returns to the records list; and Valider with a check icon, filled, which creates the record and opens it. Gone: the mid-form "Démarrer un brouillon avec ces informations", the bottom "Nouvel état des lieux · Entrée", the line "Seul votre nom est requis pour démarrer", the "Responsable du traitement" line and block with its "Vérifiez ce responsable avant de commencer", the "Facultatif : les adresses saisies sont envoyées à swisstopo" disclosure and its switch, the helper under the street field, and the agency explanation under the company field. The lookup-failed line is a status, "Recherche d'adresse indisponible.", not an instruction.

Dossier. The address card has no switch and no disclosure; the lookup behaves as above. The participant notice panel, with its share button and its "Information remise avant de recueillir les données" toggle, is gone. Inspection. The room picker has no sentence above the room chips. Date and time fields. The "Rétablir la dernière date valide" and "…heure valide" keys are undo icon buttons with that text as their accessible name.

Legal panel. "Trouver l'autorité de conciliation compétente" is "Autorité de conciliation".
