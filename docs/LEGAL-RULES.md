# Dated Swiss legal guidance

The Android rules are a structured recording aid for residential tenancies. They do not certify a contract, calculate liability or replace the competent conciliation authority. Users can preserve an unresolved check with an explanation. The original [audit](audits/2026-10-13/REPORT.md) contains the source review and its limitations.

`ch-tenancy-2026.2` was checked on 13 October 2026. Initial-rent-form guidance uses the lease conclusion date and the [BWO 2026 overview](https://www.bwo.admin.ch/fr/formule-officielle-loyer-initial). Missing dates and other years produce a verification outcome. “None listed” means no general cantonal form duty in that snapshot, not an exemption from federal tenancy law or other contractual obligations.

Neuchâtel uses the separate declared habitable-room category, excluding half rooms. The recognized municipalities and exceptional ranges follow the [2026 decree](https://rsn.ne.ch/DATA/program/books/rsne/htm/846.02.htm), linked through the [cantonal implementation law](https://rsn.ne.ch/DATA/program/books/rsne/htm/224.1.htm). An unrecognized or misspelled locality remains unresolved; it never automatically becomes exempt. Vaud uses an explicit district selection and its [2026 shortage determination](https://www.vd.ch/territoire-et-construction/logement/politique-du-logement/penurie-de-logements).

Deposit sources are [Fribourg LABLF](https://bdlf.fr.ch/app/fr/texts_of_law/222.3.1), [Geneva LGFL](https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_i4_10.htm), [Ticino implementation law](https://www3.ti.ch/CAN/RLeggi/public/index.php/raccolta-leggi/legge/num/153) and the [Vaud RULV](https://www.vd.ch/territoire-et-construction/logement/droit-du-bail). The federal [conciliation directory](https://www.bwo.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/tzHM7tFgxwhW/20260114_Schlichtungsbeh%C3%B6rden.pdf) is linked for all cantons. Opening a directory or a source does not submit a legal notice or a court filing.

Finalization freezes the resolved classification, requirement list, rule-set identifier and source links. Exact exported files are retained. Existing records without a snapshot are legacy records; their fingerprints remain valid, but their original historical PDF cannot be reconstructed byte for byte if it was never retained. Import that original as a supporting document when needed.

Maintain dated fixtures when rules change. Review the annual BWO and vacancy publications, cantonal deposit provisions and RULV effective periods. Do not extend a year-limited classification silently. Legal sign-off and native-language review remain separate from passing the automated rule tests.
